About Us

Warm Light Cafe was established in 2018, nestled in a quiet alley in the city center. We are dedicated to serving high-quality hand-brewed coffee and providing a cozy space for relaxation, allowing every visitor to find a moment of peace in their busy lives.

Handmade Pastries

Freshly baked cakes and cookies every day — the perfect companion to your coffee.

Cozy Atmosphere

Warm lighting, comfortable sofas — ideal for reading, working, or catching up with friends.
